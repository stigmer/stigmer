# Services Agreement

This agreement is between Harbor Lane Studio ("the Provider") and the
company signing below ("the Client"). It starts on the date the Client
signs it.

## 1. Services

The Provider will design and build the Client's customer portal, as set
out in the attached statement of work.

## 2. Fees

The Client will pay $48,000, in four monthly instalments of $12,000. The
Provider may change its rates at any time by updating its website, and
the new rates apply to work not yet invoiced.

## 3. Payment

Invoices are due within 10 days. Any amount not paid on time carries a
late fee of 5% per month, compounded.

## 4. Term and renewal

This agreement runs for 12 months and then renews automatically for
another 12 months unless the Client gives written notice at least 90
days before the end of the term.

## 5. Ownership of work

Everything the Provider makes under this agreement belongs to the
Provider. The Provider grants the Client a licence to use it while this
agreement is in force. Any materials the Client supplies become the
Provider's property once delivered.

## 6. Confidentiality

Each side will keep the other's confidential information secret and use
it only for this agreement, during the term and for two years after.

## 7. Liability

The Client is liable for any loss the Provider suffers in connection with
this agreement, without limit. The Provider's total liability to the
Client is limited to the fees paid in the month before the claim.

## 8. Termination

The Provider may end this agreement at any time with 7 days' notice. The
Client may end it only if the Provider has failed to deliver for 60 days
in a row, and must still pay every remaining instalment.

## 9. Governing law

This agreement is governed by the laws of the place where the Provider
has its main office.
