A Team is a named group of an organization's people that access is shared
with as one. Sharing an agent, a skill or a channel with a team
gives every member the access the share names; someone who joins the team
gains it, and someone who leaves the team or the organization loses it at
once.

A team belongs to its organization, and only the organization's members can be
in it. People join and leave a team through the Grant service, by being
granted or revoked the `member` role on it. Teams are served by Stigmer
Enterprise and Stigmer Cloud.

```yaml
apiVersion: iam.stigmer.ai/v1
kind: Team
metadata:
  name: Site Reliability
  slug: sre
  org: acme
spec:
  description: On-call engineers who run the production agents.
```

### create

Create a team in an organization.

On Stigmer Cloud, teams are a plan feature. An organization whose plan lacks them is refused with FAILED_PRECONDITION carrying a google.rpc.ErrorInfo detail (domain "stigmer.ai"):

- PLAN_UPGRADE_REQUIRED — the organization's plan does not include the feature. Metadata: feature ("teams"), org.

On Stigmer Enterprise, a new team is growth the server's license pays for. Once the license and its grace period have ended, a create is refused with FAILED_PRECONDITION carrying a google.rpc.ErrorInfo detail (domain "stigmer.ai"), before anything is written:

- LICENSE_RENEWAL_REQUIRED — the server's license has ended; every team that exists keeps working. No metadata.

A slug another team of the organization holds is refused with ALREADY_EXISTS. A slug another team was renamed away from, which still leads to it for 30 days, is refused with ALREADY_EXISTS carrying a google.rpc.ErrorInfo detail (domain "stigmer.ai"):

- SLUG_RESERVED — another team held the slug until a recent rename, and it still leads there. Metadata: slug.

### update

Update a team: the fields update_mask names change, every other field keeps its stored value.

A team's name and description may change.

resource.metadata.id names the team; apply finds one by slug. The slug changes only through rename, and a mask naming a field another door owns, or a path the team does not have, is refused with INVALID_ARGUMENT naming the path.

An update is refused with ABORTED carrying a google.rpc.ErrorInfo detail (domain "stigmer.ai"):

- ETAG_MISMATCH — resource.metadata.etag is set and the team changed since that etag was read: read it again and make the change again. No metadata. - WRITE_CONFLICT — the team kept changing while the update was being saved: try again. No metadata.

### rename

Rename a team: change its slug, the name people type.

The team keeps its id, so everything that names it keeps working, and every answer names it by the new slug. The old slug keeps leading to the team for reading for 30 days, during which no other team of the organization can take it and this one can take it back; then it is released. A slug another team holds is refused with ALREADY_EXISTS, and one another team was recently renamed away from with SLUG_RESERVED (see create).

Its memberships and shares name its id and are unchanged.
