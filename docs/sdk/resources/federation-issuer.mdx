A FederationIssuer is an outside issuer whose tokens Stigmer accepts for its
own users. It configures how Stigmer validates the tokens that issuer's users
present on each request, and how their federated identity accounts are created.
Served by the Enterprise and Cloud editions; the open-source server answers
UNIMPLEMENTED.

```yaml
apiVersion: iam.stigmer.ai/v1
kind: FederationIssuer
metadata:
  name: Planton
  slug: planton
  org: planton
spec:
  display_name: "Planton"
  jwks_uri: "https://planton-prod.us.auth0.com/.well-known/jwks.json"
  allowed_issuers: ["https://planton-prod.us.auth0.com/"]
  expected_audience: "https://api.planton.ai/"
  userinfo_endpoint: "https://planton-prod.us.auth0.com/userinfo"
```

### update

Update a federation issuer: the fields update_mask names change, every other field keeps its stored value.

On Stigmer Enterprise, changing how people sign in is growth the server's license pays for. Once the license and its grace period have ended, an update is refused with FAILED_PRECONDITION carrying a google.rpc.ErrorInfo detail (domain "stigmer.ai"), before anything is written:

- LICENSE_RENEWAL_REQUIRED — the server's license has ended; the federation issuer keeps signing people in as it is, and can still be deleted. No metadata.

resource.metadata.id names the federation issuer; apply finds one by slug. The slug changes only through rename, and a mask naming a field another door owns, or a path the federation issuer does not have, is refused with INVALID_ARGUMENT naming the path.

An update is refused with ABORTED carrying a google.rpc.ErrorInfo detail (domain "stigmer.ai"):

- ETAG_MISMATCH — resource.metadata.etag is set and the federation issuer changed since that etag was read: read it again and make the change again. No metadata. - WRITE_CONFLICT — the federation issuer kept changing while the update was being saved: try again. No metadata.

### rename

Rename a federation issuer: change its slug, the name people type.

The federation issuer keeps its id, so everything that names it keeps working, and every answer names it by the new slug. The old slug keeps leading to the federation issuer for reading for 30 days, during which no other federation issuer of the organization can take it and this one can take it back; then it is released. A write that names the old slug is refused with SLUG_RENAMED (see apply). A slug another federation issuer holds is refused with ALREADY_EXISTS, and one another federation issuer was recently renamed away from with SLUG_RESERVED (see create).

Sign-in does not name its slug, so the people it signs in keep signing in.

### IamRole

IamRole defines the roles that can be assigned to principals on resources via Grants.

| Value | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| `owner` | Full control of the resource, including deleting it and deciding who has access. |
| `admin` | Administers an organization: its people, settings and resources, short of its owners, its deletion and its private conversations. |
| `member` | Belongs to an organization or a team. |
| `viewer` | Sees the resource and changes nothing. |
| `participant` | A person who takes part in a conversation: reads it and sends messages. |
| `editor` | Editor of a blueprint (an agent): may change its definition and do whatever a viewer can, and may not delete it, change its visibility, give it a chat link or another channel, or decide who else has access. |
| `user` | May use a shared vault: the runs of a person who holds it use the vault's logins and secrets. |
