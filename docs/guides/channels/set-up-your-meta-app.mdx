---
title: Set Up Your Meta App
description:
  Register your Meta app in Stigmer so an Agent can serve a WhatsApp number.
  Every WhatsApp channel connects through an app you own — this setup comes
  first.
---

WhatsApp channels always connect through your own Meta app. Unlike Slack, there
is no shared platform app to fall back on: Meta's WhatsApp Cloud API ties every
number to the business that owns it, so Stigmer serves your number through your
app's credentials. You register those credentials once as a **Channel App** —
then any Agent in your Organization can connect a WhatsApp number through it.

<Callout type="info">
  This is a prerequisite for [Connect an Agent to
  WhatsApp](/docs/guides/channels/connect-whatsapp). The connect flow requires a
  registered Meta app — with none in your Organization, it asks you to register
  one first.
</Callout>

## What you need

- A [Meta Business portfolio](https://business.facebook.com/) with **complete
  business information** — legal name, address, phone, website, and a
  **confirmed** business email. A freshly created portfolio starts empty and
  Meta refuses to create a WhatsApp Business account until these are filled in
  (the error mentions "policy requirements"). Fill them under **Business
  settings → Business info** and confirm the verification email before you
  start.
- A phone number for the Agent that is **not registered on any WhatsApp mobile
  app** (personal or business). A number on the Cloud API cannot also live on a
  phone's WhatsApp.
- A **live privacy policy URL** on your website. Meta requires it to publish the
  app, and an unpublished app does not receive production webhooks.
- Permission to manage Channel Apps in your Stigmer Organization.

Two different Meta sites are involved, and it helps to know which is which:
**business.facebook.com** (Business settings) owns your WhatsApp Business
account — the WABA — with its phone number, and your system users;
**developers.facebook.com** owns the Meta _app_, which has the App ID, App
secret, and webhook configuration. Stigmer talks to WhatsApp through the app;
the app is attached to your WABA.

## Step 1 — Create the WhatsApp Business account and number

In [Business settings](https://business.facebook.com/settings), under **Accounts
→ WhatsApp accounts**, click **Add → Create a new WhatsApp Business account**
(not "Link" — that option is for accounts tied to the WhatsApp mobile app). Pick
a display name — this is the name people see when the Agent replies — and any
business category; both are cosmetic and changeable later.

Add your phone number to the account and verify it with the SMS or voice code
Meta sends. If the creation wizard skips the number step, add it afterwards from
the account's **Phone numbers** tab.

## Step 2 — Create the Meta app and attach the account

1. On [developers.facebook.com](https://developers.facebook.com/apps), create an
   app. When the wizard asks what your app does, pick the use case that mentions
   **WhatsApp** ("Connect with customers through WhatsApp"), and link the app to
   your business portfolio when prompted.
2. Meta's app dashboard changes often. In the current use-case layout, the
   WhatsApp configuration lives under **Use cases → Customize → Integrate with
   API**. Ignore "Become a Partner" / "Become a Tech Provider" — that track is
   for companies serving other businesses and drags you into App Review.
3. Open **Step 2 — Production setup** in that flow and make sure your real
   WhatsApp Business account and number are the ones attached (not the test
   account Meta creates automatically). Skip the webhook section for now — the
   webhook address comes from Stigmer later. Skip the payment method too:
   Stigmer's Agent only ever _replies_ to people who message it, and service
   replies need no payment method.
4. From **App settings → Basic**, copy the **App ID** and **App secret**.

## Step 3 — Publish the app

While the app is unpublished, Meta delivers only test webhooks — real messages
are silently dropped, even for app admins. Publish before wiring anything to
Stigmer:

1. In **App settings → Basic**, set the **Privacy Policy URL** to a live page on
   your site and save.
2. Open **Publish** in the left sidebar and publish the app. For an app serving
   your own business's WhatsApp account, no App Review is involved.

<Callout type="warn">
  This is the easiest step to miss, and the failure is invisible: the webhook
  verifies, outbound sends work, but incoming messages never arrive. If your
  Agent stays silent later, check the app's publish state first.
</Callout>

## Step 4 — Create a permanent access token

Create the token from a system user in Meta
[Business settings](https://business.facebook.com/settings) — under **Users →
System users**:

1. Add a system user (any name, for example `stigmer-runner`), role **Admin**.
2. On the system user, **Add assets** twice: your **app** (full control) and
   your **WhatsApp account** (full control).
3. Click **Generate new token**: select your app, set token expiration to
   **Never**, and grant at least **whatsapp_business_messaging** and
   **whatsapp_business_management**. The exact permission list Meta offers
   depends on the app's use case — `business_management` may not appear at all,
   and extras like `whatsapp_business_manage_events` are harmless.
4. Copy the token — Meta shows it once.

<Callout type="warn">
  Do not use the temporary access token from the app dashboard's setup pages. It
  expires within a day, and WhatsApp gives no revocation signal — the channel
  keeps looking healthy until a reply fails to send. The system-user token does
  not expire.
</Callout>

## Step 5 — Register the app in Stigmer

1. In the web console, open **Settings → Channel Apps** and click **Register
   channel app**.
2. Under **Provider**, pick **WhatsApp**.
3. Enter the **Name**. It identifies the app inside Stigmer — for example
   `Acme WhatsApp`.
4. Paste the **App ID**, **App secret**, and **Access token** from steps 2
   and 4. Stigmer encrypts the secrets at rest and never shows them again.
5. Keep the generated **Verify token** — a strong random value Meta will echo
   back when it verifies your webhook. Replace it only if your Meta app already
   has one configured.
6. Click **Register channel app**.

## Step 6 — Finish setup in Meta

The saved Channel App now has its own webhook address — the URL Meta delivers
this app's messages to. The app's detail panel shows it under **Finish setup in
Meta**, together with the verify token from registration.

<Callout>
  The verify token is visible on this screen only once, right after
  registration. Complete this step before leaving the page — if you lose the
  token, paste a new one into the app's credentials and use that in Meta
  instead.
</Callout>

1. Copy the **Callback URL** and **Verify token** from the detail panel.
2. In the Meta app's webhook configuration (currently under **Use cases →
   Customize → Production setup → Configure Webhooks**), paste them into the
   **Callback URL** and **Verify token** fields. Leave **Attach a client
   certificate** switched off — with it on, the form refuses to save until a
   certificate is uploaded. Click **Verify and save**. Meta verifies immediately
   — verification passes because Stigmer checks the echoed token against the one
   you registered.
3. Subscribe to the **messages** webhook field (the field list is linked from
   the same webhook configuration area). This is the only field Stigmer needs —
   it carries the incoming messages your Agent answers.
4. Confirm the app is subscribed to your WhatsApp Business account. Verifying
   the callback URL does not always do this by itself, and without the
   subscription no messages are delivered. Check with the Graph API — the
   response should list your app:

```bash
curl "https://graph.facebook.com/v23.0/<WABA_ID>/subscribed_apps?access_token=<TOKEN>"
```

If the list is empty, subscribe with the same URL as a POST:

```bash
curl -X POST "https://graph.facebook.com/v23.0/<WABA_ID>/subscribed_apps?access_token=<TOKEN>"
```

Your WABA ID is shown under **Business settings → Accounts → WhatsApp
accounts**.

## Connect an Agent through the app

Registration done, the connect flow picks it up automatically: open the Agent
under **Library → Agents**, go to the **Channels** tab, and click **Connect to
WhatsApp**. The dialog offers your app under **Serving app** — with a single
registered app, it is already selected. Continue with
[Connect an Agent to WhatsApp](/docs/guides/channels/connect-whatsapp).

## Rotate credentials or remove the app

Back in **Settings → Channel Apps**, the app's detail panel manages its
lifecycle:

- **Rotate a secret** — stored secrets display as `***REDACTED***`. Leave a
  field untouched to keep its value, or paste a new one to rotate it. If you
  rotate the verify token, update Meta's webhook configuration to the same
  value.
- **Delete the app** — Stigmer refuses the deletion while any channel still
  connects through the app. Disconnect those channels first, then delete.
