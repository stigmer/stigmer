Identity federation lets users access Stigmer with tokens issued by an external
authentication system. Instead of requiring separate Stigmer accounts, you
register your auth provider as an Identity Provider and Stigmer trusts its
tokens.

Federation supports two paths:

- **Platform-managed federation** — Your platform backend provisions accounts
  and grants roles via the API. Your users call the Stigmer API with JWTs from
  your auth provider.
- **Self-managed SSO** — Your team members sign in to the Stigmer web console
  through your Organization's Identity Provider. Accounts are created
  automatically on first login.

## When you need this

You're building a platform on Stigmer and your users already authenticate
through your own OIDC-compatible provider (Auth0, Okta, Entra ID, AWS Cognito,
or any custom OIDC server). You want those users to interact with Stigmer
Agents, Sessions, and other resources without a separate Stigmer login.

## What you'll build

By the end of this guide, your platform will:

1. Have an Identity Provider registered in Stigmer that trusts your auth system
2. Provision federated accounts for your users as they sign up
3. Grant those users appropriate roles on your Organization
4. Accept API calls authenticated with JWTs from your provider

The setup happens once per user (steps 1--3). After that, every API call follows
the same path: present a JWT, Stigmer validates it against your Identity
Provider's JWKS endpoint, resolves the caller to a federated account, and checks
IAM Policies.

## Prerequisites

Before starting, you need:

- A **Stigmer Organization** (either self-managed or platform-managed)
- An **API key** with admin access to that Organization
- An **OIDC-compatible auth provider** with:
  - A JWKS endpoint URL (typically `https://your-domain/.well-known/jwks.json`)
  - The issuer URL (the `iss` claim value in your JWTs)
  - The audience value (the `aud` claim your provider puts in tokens intended
    for Stigmer)

## Self-managed SSO

If your Organization is self-managed and you want team members to sign in to the
Stigmer web console through your own Identity Provider, you can enable SSO.
Stigmer creates accounts automatically on first login and assigns a viewer role.
