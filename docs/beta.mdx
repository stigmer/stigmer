Stigmer is in Beta: things may change, and your work is kept. Tell us what
breaks.

Stigmer says this once wherever you first meet it: beside its name on this site
and in the console, on the sign-in screen, after `stigmer auth login` and
`stigmer up`, and in the setup guide your coding agent follows. This page is
what each of those links to.

## What may change

Beta means the product is still taking its shape from the developers who use it.
Between releases, any part you touch may change:

- commands and their flags in the [command line](/docs/cli);
- fields in the API and in the [SDKs](/docs/sdk);
- screens in the console and the desktop app;
- the defaults an agent runs with.

Each release says what changed in its [release notes](/releases). Read them
before you upgrade a server you run yourself.

## What is kept

Your work outlives every change.

- **On Stigmer Cloud**, your agents, sessions, files and keys are kept from
  release to release. No wipe is planned.
- **On your own server or laptop**, the data is yours and stays where you put
  it. The operations guide shows how to
  [back it up](/docs/guides/self-hosting/operations#back-up),
  [restore it](/docs/guides/self-hosting/operations#restore) and
  [upgrade it](/docs/guides/self-hosting/operations#upgrade).

## Tell us what breaks

A rough edge you hit is one the team wants to hear about, whether it stopped you
or only slowed you down.

- Open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/stigmer/stigmer/issues), with
  what you ran and what you saw.
- Ask in the [Discord community](https://discord.gg/EtANnfsJ8B).
- In a console whose operator set a help link, the sidebar's help row reaches
  them.

## The bar for lifting Beta

Beta comes off when all of these hold:

- three developers outside the team set Stigmer up from the
  [coding-agent setup](/docs/getting-started/coding-agent) without the team's
  help, each ending with an agent in their own app that has called one of their
  app's own tools;
- that path has no open problem;
- every way to manage a plugin works from both the console and the command line;
- the whole journey, from signing up to the agent calling a tool, is tested on
  every change and every night against Stigmer Cloud.

Lifting Beta ships in a release whose notes say so, and this page then says so
too.
