[Run the quickstart](/docs/getting-started/quickstart).

## What happens

1. **You paste.** Your coding agent installs Stigmer's command line and signs
   you in.
2. **It asks, you choose.** It reads your app and suggests the agent that fits
   your users. Your model key never passes through the chat.
3. **It's in your app.** It runs the agent once, then adds the token route and
   the chat, and opens the page.
