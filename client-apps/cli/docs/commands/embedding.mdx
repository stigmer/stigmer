How people sign in to your Organization from your own app: whether their account
is made the first time your app signs them in, and the role it gets.

Your app's backend signs its users in to Stigmer by minting a token for each one
with an admin service account's key (the route `stigmer app-handover` writes).
While account creation is on, the server makes that person's account on their
first sign-in and gives it the sign-in role; while it is off, your app makes
accounts first. `set` changes those two and keeps everything else as it stands
(the allowed origins and the vaults), which the console's Settings change. A
role needs account creation on, so turning creation off clears the role. Only
the Organization's admins can change these.

{/* AUTO_USAGE */}

### Common patterns

```bash
# The settings as they stand
stigmer embedding

# Make an app user's account on their first sign-in, as a Member
stigmer embedding set --create-accounts-on-sign-in on --sign-in-role member

# Your app makes accounts itself
stigmer embedding set --create-accounts-on-sign-in off
```
