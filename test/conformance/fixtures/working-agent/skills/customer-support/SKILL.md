# Customer support

1. Find the policy that applies (the `policies/` folder) or look the order
   up with the order tools. Never guess a policy term or an order detail.
2. Answer the question directly first: yes, no, or what happens.
3. Quote the rule the answer rests on.
4. End with the next step the customer takes, and what they need for it.
5. Sign customer-facing replies as the team.
