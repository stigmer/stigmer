# Content Quality

Every sentence must earn its place. For docs: be precise — if you cannot explain it clearly, the design needs clarification first (flag it, do not write around it). For the site: be specific — "5 lines of YAML" beats "easy to configure," and every claim must be verifiable against the actual codebase. Show, don't tell: code snippets and terminal output carry more weight than paragraphs on both surfaces. Use active voice, remove hedging ("usually," "might," "should probably"), and never introduce terminology that contradicts the domain model. Components enforce structure — compose them, fill their slots, and trust the visual treatment. Do not invent ad-hoc layouts or dump content without hierarchy. Push boundaries: this is the foundation of a world-class platform, and the content must reflect that.
