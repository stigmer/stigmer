# Meridian Travel Rebooking Policy

This policy governs every itinerary change on a Meridian Travel booking. Apply it exactly: quote the traveler the full cost of a change before executing it, and never waive a fee this document does not waive.

## 1. When a traveler may rebook

A traveler may move a booking to any Meridian-operated flight on the same route. Route changes are new bookings, not rebookings. A rebooking always prices against the traveler's original fare class — never silently upgrade or downgrade.

## 2. Change fees by fare class

| Fare class | Change fee | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Saver | $99 | Fee applies per rebooking, per traveler |
| Standard | $49 | Fee applies per rebooking, per traveler |
| Flex | $0 | Unlimited free changes |

The change fee is charged in addition to any fare difference.

## 3. Fare difference

- If the new flight's fare (in the traveler's fare class) is **higher** than what they paid, charge the difference.
- If it is **lower**, refund the difference as travel credit. The change fee is never refunded.
- Always state the fare difference and the change fee as separate line items, then the total.

## 4. Disruption handling (Meridian-caused changes)

When Meridian cancels or significantly delays a flight (more than 3 hours), the rebooking is free for every fare class: no change fee, and no fare difference on the same route within 7 days of the original travel date. Identify these as "disruption rebookings" in the confirmation.

## 5. Communication rules

- Confirm the traveler's booking reference before quoting anything.
- Quote all costs in USD, itemized: fare difference, change fee, total.
- After executing a rebooking, give the traveler the new confirmation code, the full new itinerary, and the amount charged or credited.
