The Stigmer operator {{ include "stigmer-operator.version" . }} is installed in {{ .Release.Namespace }}.

Declare Stigmer in a namespace of its own, with the Secret that holds your
model provider's key:

  kubectl create namespace stigmer
  kubectl -n stigmer create secret generic anthropic --from-literal=ANTHROPIC_API_KEY=<your key>
  kubectl apply -f - <<'YAML'
  apiVersion: stigmer.ai/v1
  kind: Stigmer
  metadata:
    name: stigmer
    namespace: stigmer
  spec:
    owner:
      email: you@example.com
    runner:
      llm:
        secretName: anthropic
  YAML
  kubectl -n stigmer get stigmer
