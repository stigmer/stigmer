Judge the answer on these points alone. It passes when it makes every Must point and does nothing a FAIL-if line names. Leaving out a Welcome point never fails it, and a correct point not listed here is no fault.

**Must**

- Says staff take over a conversation in the console's **Conversations** area on Stigmer Cloud (for example **Take over**, or simply replying), which makes the Agent go quiet there while their reply goes out through the same number.
- Says **Hand back to the agent** returns the conversation: the Agent resumes on the customer's next message and receives a digest of what happened while it was quiet, so it does not repeat settled answers.
- Says replying, taking over and handing back need the **Participant** role on the channel.
- Says Slack does not work the same: staff replies work on WhatsApp channels today, while Slack conversations can be read but only the Agent replies there.

**FAIL if**

- It tells them to build their own handoff or inbox.
- It invents an API, command or setting the docs don't name.
- It says staff can reply in Slack conversations from the console.
- It says handing back makes the Agent answer immediately.

**Welcome**

- The **Needs a person** list and the sidebar badge show conversations that want a human, including ones the Agent escalated on its own (with its reason), and **Take over** or **Dismiss** answers the flag.
- A customer who writes before anyone replies gets one acknowledgment ("Someone from our team is looking at this and will reply here.").
- Reading conversations needs view access on the channel.
- WhatsApp's 24-hour window for free-form replies, with approved templates for reaching out past it.
- Handing back warns when the customer's last message has no reply.
- Participant is granted from the channel card's **Manage access**.
