## Decisions Made

1. **ConnectBar is internal (not exported)** — a standalone ConnectBar without the detail view context has limited utility for platform builders. The `useMcpServerConnect` hook is the headless integration point.

2. **Full transparency in Policies tab** — if a tool appears in both `spec.pinnedToolApprovals` and `status.toolApprovals`, both entries are shown in their respective visual groups (Pinned vs Auto-classified). No deduplication. Users see exactly what the system computed vs what they manually set.

3. **No migration/deprecation path for `useDiscoverCapabilities`** — pre-1.0 platform, clean rename where the underlying RPC is unchanged.

4. **Credential flow unchanged** — the ConnectBar reuses the same `useMcpServerCredentials` hook and `EnvVarForm` component. UX is identical, just relocated from inside the Tools tab to above the tabs.

5. **Demo fixture renamed** — `fixtures.mcpServer.discoverCapabilities` → `fixtures.mcpServer.connect` to match the RPC name.
