## Decisions Made

1. **No enricher needed**: Discovered that query-time BYOA enrichment is not feasible because the get-by-ID pipeline has no caller org context. The existing `getOrgOAuthApp` RPC (explicit org parameter) serves the frontend's needs. Frontend composes effective source from three calls.

2. **Query RPC placement**: Moved getOAuthGrantStatus and getOrgOAuthApp to McpServerQueryController — both are read-only with can_view authorization, not commands. Authorization stays on mcp_server resource (not organization) to prevent info leaks about resources the caller can't see.

3. **Defensive fallthrough**: If an override's OAuthApp is deleted, the resolution service logs a warning and falls through to platform default instead of hard-failing.
