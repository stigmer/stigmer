## Decisions Made

- **Skip `doc-coauthoring`**: No LICENSE.txt or license frontmatter field. Without explicit Apache 2.0 grant, redistribution in the seedpack (embedded in CLI binary) carries legal risk. Track as follow-up.
- **Skip document skills** (docx, pdf, pptx, xlsx): Anthropic license explicitly prohibits redistribution/sublicensing. Confirmed by reading per-skill LICENSE.txt.
- **Add `canvas-design` and `frontend-design`**: Not in original plan but both Apache 2.0 and high-value for the platform. Expanded from 6 to 7 vendored skills.
- **Accept `canvas-design` font weight**: 5.5 MB of .ttf files in canvas-fonts/. Accepted for now; future optimization candidate (on-demand download vs. Go embed).
- **Single commit SHA for all skills**: All 8 vendored skills pinned to `12ab35c2eb5668c95810e6a6066f40f4218adc39` for consistency.

- The vendor script had a latent bug that never manifested because only one skill was ever vendored. The `cd` into the temp dir + `rm -rf` at function end left the shell in a deleted directory for subsequent iterations.

- The skill-creator upstream has changed significantly since last vendor (March 8): new agents/, assets/, eval-viewer/ directories; several scripts added/removed.
