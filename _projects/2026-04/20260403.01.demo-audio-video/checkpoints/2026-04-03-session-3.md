## Decisions Made

- **Narration rhythm**: Not every step gets narration. Navigation, scrolling, and cursor clicks are visual pauses — silence lets the viewer's eyes process before the next concept lands. Narrate concepts and outcomes, not mechanics.
- **Narration density**: ~40% of steps narrated (30/75). Each narrated step is 1–2 sentences. Steps without narration are deliberate silence, not omissions.
- **Batch-and-review workflow**: Narration was written in four batches (Quickstart, Creation tours, MCP features, Concept demos) with human review after each batch to maintain quality.
- **Differentiation across tours**: The three 12-step creation tours (Skill, Agent, MCP server) share identical UI structure. Each tour's narration emphasizes different concepts to avoid formulaic repetition: Skill=domain knowledge, Agent=blueprint composition, MCP server=external connections.
- **Document writer guidelines**: Kept the narration section to ~15 lines, parallel in structure to the cursor overlay section. Excluded build pipeline details, timing mechanics, and voice selection — the writer just writes text, the engine handles the rest.

## Learnings

- The smoke-test narration field from Phase 2 (`"SMOKE TEST — delete..."`) on `quickstart-playback` step 0 was already cleaned up by the Phase 2 session — no conflict to resolve.
- Caption and narration serve fundamentally different roles: caption is a visual subtitle (3–8 words), narration is spoken context (1–2 sentences). Writing narration as if it were a longer caption produces poor results — narration must add understanding the caption cannot carry.
- The generate-policies and discover-capabilities demos form a narrative pair. Treating them as a continuous story (connect → discover → realize risk → add safety) is more effective than narrating them independently.
