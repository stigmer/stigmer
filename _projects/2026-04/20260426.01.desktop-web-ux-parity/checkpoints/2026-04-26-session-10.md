## Key Findings from Parity Verification

All library and settings pages are structurally identical across web and desktop. Differences are exclusively framework-specific:
- `next/link` vs `react-router-dom/Link`
- `useRouter` / `usePathname` vs `useNavigate` / `useLocation`
- `useStaticRouteParam` vs `useParams`
- SSR guard in web's `readPersistedScope` (desktop omits — no SSR in Tauri)
- Web's `LibraryNavigationProvider` (SPA-within-a-page) vs desktop's `Outlet`-based routing
