## Learnings

- The original task plan underestimated the coupling between sync and snapshot workflows. What was listed as "delete files + clean up config" turned out to be a careful refactoring exercise because both workflows shared the same worker config, config properties class, and task queue.
- The `isRegistrySynced()` filter in the snapshot builder was a hidden dependency on the sync workflow that would have caused silent failure (snapshot builder would silently fall back to baseline-only packages).
