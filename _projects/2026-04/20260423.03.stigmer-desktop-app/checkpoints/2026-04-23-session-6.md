## Learnings

- `tauri-plugin-updater` must be registered in `setup` closure, not the builder chain
- `relaunch()` requires `tauri-plugin-process` as a separate dependency
- `tauri signer generate` requires interactive TTY; `expect` works for automation
- `createUpdaterArtifacts` must be `true` (not `"v1Compatible"`) for new v2 apps
- Go sidecar requires `-tags embed_agentrunner` for runner start; `embed_webconsole` not needed (desktop has its own UI)
