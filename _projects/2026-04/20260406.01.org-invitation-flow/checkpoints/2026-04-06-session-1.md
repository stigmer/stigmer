## Decisions Made

- **can_execute separation**: Discovered that `can_execute: viewer` on agent.fga and workflow.fga would leak cost exposure to org viewers. Resolved by explicitly enumerating subjects: `[identity_account, identity_account:*] or owner or member from organization`. This preserves public agent execution and org member execution while excluding org viewers.
- **can_clone left as-is**: `can_clone: viewer` remains unchanged — clone at source only grants permission to copy, the destination org's `can_create_*` permission acts as a secondary guard. Org viewers lack `can_create_*`.
- **can_use left as-is**: `can_use: viewer` on skill/mcp_server remains unchanged — these are used when building agent configs, not direct user actions. Org viewers can't create agents.
- **T01 open questions resolved**: All 5 open questions from T01 plan were approved with their recommended answers (block platform-managed orgs, token in InvitationStatus, configurable max expiry, invitations not searchable, InvitationPreview info disclosure acceptable).

## Learnings

- FGA model changes that widen a relation (member → viewer from org) can have cascading effects on computed permissions (can_execute, can_clone) that derive from that relation. Always trace the full permission graph when modifying relations.
- The `iam-role.ts` and `RoleSelector` components were already designed for extensibility — adding a new role required zero manual SDK changes.
