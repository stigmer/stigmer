## Decisions Made

- **Normal links, not disabled items**: Placeholder nav items look and behave identically to real items — same styling, hover effects, click behavior. The "coming soon" message lives on the destination page, not in the sidebar. This was a deliberate UX choice over the initial muted/disabled approach.
- **Separate URLs per section**: Each placeholder gets its own route (`/settings/billing`, etc.) instead of a shared `/settings/coming-soon` route. This ensures sidebar active-state highlighting works correctly and establishes URLs that won't need to change when features ship.
- **Shared component, thin pages**: A single `ComingSoon` component accepts `title` and optional `icon` props. Each route page is a 5-line wrapper. When a feature ships, replace the page content — no sidebar or routing changes needed.

## Learnings
