## Decisions Made

- **Single-consumer views should be inlined**: `ApiKeysView` was only used by one scenario. Inlining it reduces file count and removes unnecessary indirection. Multi-consumer views earn their existence.
- **ManagementShell is a separate component from AppShell**: Different nav items, different props (`ManagementNavId` vs `NavId`), no widget aside. Mirrors the real Console architecture (`Sidebar` vs `ManagementSidebar`).
- **SDK components imported directly in scenario**: `ApiKeyListPanel`, `CreateApiKeyForm`, `ApiKeyCreatedAlert` come from `@stigmer/react` — no wrapper needed.
