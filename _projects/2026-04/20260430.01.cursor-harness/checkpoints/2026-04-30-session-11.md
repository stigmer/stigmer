## Key Decisions
- Created a dedicated `release.cursor-runner.yaml` workflow rather than adding to `publish-libs.mjs` — the cursor-runner has different publishing needs (native Temporal deps, bin entry) vs SDK packages
- Chose Option A (extend existing sandbox image) over separate image — simpler operationally, avoids image selection plumbing in stigmer-service
- Node.js 22 LTS via multi-stage COPY from `node:22-slim` rather than NodeSource apt repo — deterministic, no external apt sources
