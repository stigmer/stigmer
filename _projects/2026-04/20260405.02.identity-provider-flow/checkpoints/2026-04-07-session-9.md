## Decisions Made

- **Not Getting Started**: Identity federation is a different audience moment from "zero to first agent." It belongs in a task-oriented Guides section, not Getting Started.
- **Multi-page, not single page**: The federation flow spans 3 resources (IdentityProvider, IdentityAccount, IamPolicy), 3 personas, and multiple steps. Split into 5 focused pages.
- **"Guides" not "Tutorials"**: CONTRIBUTING.md envisioned `tutorials/`, but "Guides" better matches Diataxis — these are task-oriented how-tos, not learning exercises.
- **Concepts page bridges the gap**: `docs/concepts/identity.mdx` explains the model; guide pages explain the procedure. No duplication with auto-generated SDK reference.
- **Vale vocabulary additions**: Added JWTs, JWT, JWKS, OIDC, PKCE, Okta, Cognito, Entra, IdP, IdPs, signup, onboarding to accept.txt.
- **`<!-- vale off/on -->` for Mermaid blocks**: Mermaid chart content inside `<Mermaid chart={...}/>` triggers false positives (sequenceDiagram, node IDs). Wrapped with HTML comments.

## Learnings

- Vale lints MDX content mapped as MD, including content inside JSX attributes (Mermaid charts). Use `<!-- vale off/on -->` to suppress false positives.
- Prettier reformats files in pre-commit (via lint-staged), then Vale lints. Line numbers in Vale errors correspond to prettier-formatted output, not original.
- The `TokenIgnores` regex in `.vale.ini` strips JSX opening tags but not their attribute content.
