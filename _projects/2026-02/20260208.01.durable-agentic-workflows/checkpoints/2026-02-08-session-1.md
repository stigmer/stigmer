## Critical Discovery

**Activity retries are disabled** in `InvokeAgentExecutionWorkflowImpl.java`:
```java
.setMaximumAttempts(1)  // No retries - "agent execution not idempotent"
```

This means the system **fails instead of recovering** from crashes. This is a defensive workaround, not true durability. Fixing Gap A1 (checkpoint resume) and A2 (tool idempotency) will allow enabling retries for actual crash recovery.

## Decisions Made

   - Can't enable retries without both
   - They unlock the "durable agentic workflows" claim

2. **Implementation order**:
