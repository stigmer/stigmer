## Decisions Made

- **No Auth0 creation for federated users**: Federated identity accounts exist only in Stigmer's data layer (MongoDB, Redis, FGA), not in Auth0. Auth0 is for direct users only.
- **No proxy needed**: External platforms call Stigmer gRPC APIs directly with their own Auth0 tokens.
- **No auto-grant of org membership**: Authorization is the consuming platform's responsibility.
- **Audience validation at decoder creation time**: Not per-request (thread safety fix for `NimbusJwtDecoder.setJwtValidator`).
- **`@Qualifier("additionalAuthProviders")`**: Prevents Spring from collecting all `AuthenticationProvider` beans when injecting `List<AuthenticationProvider>`.
