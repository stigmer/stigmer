## Decisions Made

- **Scope**: Focused on list/collection tables only; get/detail views (key-value) out of scope
- **Separator style**: ASCII dashes (`---`) for broader terminal compatibility over box-drawing characters (`───`)
- **Adaptive algorithm**: Widest-first shrinking (generalized) over apply table's last-column-first heuristic
- **`truncateString` consolidation**: Treated as correctness bug (not cosmetic), since byte-based `s[:n]` silently breaks for multi-byte Unicode
- **`DisplayEmptyResults` delegation**: `search.DisplayEmptyResults` remains as a thin wrapper to minimize blast radius on existing callers (agent, workflow)
- **Pagination bug**: Discovered but explicitly NOT fixed — session/execution hardcode "Page 1 of %d". Flagged as future work (touches command-handler signatures)
- **No pre-truncation of data**: Table's adaptive mode handles cell trimming; callers no longer pre-truncate values
