## Key Decisions Made

- **`mcp-servers` authority (hyphenated)**: First hyphenated authority in `kindToAuthority`. URIs are conventional; hyphens are standard in URI path components. Clean mapping from `mcp_server` (proto enum) to `mcp-servers` (URI authority).
- **Tool included alongside template**: Option B's spec mentioned only the resource template, but consistency with `agents`/`skills`/`workflows` required a `get_mcp_server` tool too. Zero cost once `Fetch` exists; omitting it would leave an asymmetry with no benefit.
- **No versioned template**: `mcp_server` is `is_versioned: false` in `kind_meta`. Correct to follow the agents/workflows pattern (single unversioned template only).
