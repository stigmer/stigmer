## Learnings

- Auto-apply Cursor rules with globs are the strongest enforcement mechanism — they fire without any user action when matching files are in context
- Rules that just "point to" other files don't enforce anything — the AI treats them as optional reading
- The most critical enforcement content should be inlined in auto-apply rules, not delegated to manual-load files
- Reminders are best for cross-cutting working-style concerns (plan-first, collaboration), not for file-type-specific enforcement
