## Decisions Made

1. **Diataxis governs page content, not navigation**: Every page is exactly one type (tutorial, how-to, explanation, reference), but the sidebar organizes by reader journey, not by content type. This matches industry best practice (Stripe, Vercel, Supabase) and avoids scattering related content across four type-based sections.

2. **Journey-then-topic sidebar**: Getting Started at the top (entry), Concepts and Tutorials in the middle (learning), SDKs/CLI/Reference at the bottom (integration). Two `---Label---` separators ("Learn" and "Integrate") group the middle and bottom sections.

3. **Concepts ordered by pillar progression**: what-is-stigmer → agents → skills → tools → approval-flows → sessions → workflows → environments → organizations. This mirrors the positioning's progressive disclosure (knowledge → tools → approvals) before expanding to supporting concepts.

4. **Cloud quickstart first**: Follows positioning Decision 3. Self-hosted quickstart appears second as a trust signal.

5. **Three document writer role corrections identified**: Writing-context awareness (replace absolute plain-language rule with context-sensitive calibration), Diataxis scope clarification (per-page quality, not sidebar mandate), infrastructure-analogy prohibition scoped to sales/introductory docs.

6. **Future IA revisions anticipated**: Per-topic directories when a concept accumulates 3+ pages, and a dedicated how-to section when 4+ standalone how-to pages exist. These are growth signals, not current needs.
