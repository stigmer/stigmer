## Decisions Made

- **Naming**: Dropped `-Widget` suffix from the original plan. Used `ExecutionSummary`, `ContextWindowMeter`, `WorkspaceSummary` to follow established SDK naming conventions where suffixes describe the UI metaphor.
- **ExecutionSummary scope**: Expanded beyond original plan's "phase + duration" to include model + tokens + cost. Rationale: these are commonly co-needed observability metrics; `ExecutionPhaseBadge` already covers the phase-only case.
- **No card chrome**: Components render content without border/background/elevation — consumer controls the container. Avoids style tension between `ExecutionDetails` (section dividers) and session page (card containers).
- **Domain placement**: `WorkspaceSummary` in `workspace/` domain, not `execution/` — workspace entries are a session-level concept from `session.spec.workspaceEntries`.
- **ResolvedContextSection not extracted**: Kept internal to `ExecutionDetails` — too niche for standalone use.
- **Shared utilities not exported**: `execution-format.ts` is internal to the execution domain, not in barrel exports.

## Learnings

- The `ExecutionDetails` component had zero consumers in the Console or anywhere else — it was library-only. This made refactoring low-risk.
- The workspace domain already had `WorkspaceEditor` and `FolderBrowser`, making it the natural home for `WorkspaceSummary`.
- The protobuf `WorkspaceEntry` type (from session workspace proto) is different from the SDK's local `WorkspaceEntry` interface in `useWorkspaceEntries.ts`. The proto type is the right one for `WorkspaceSummary` since it represents session-level data.
