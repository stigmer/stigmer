## Learnings

- The priority cascade is now fully consistent: `approvalActive`, `streamingActive`, and `aiStreamActive` all show sub-agent lines above their primary content. Only `spinnerActive` (lowest priority) does not, which is correct because `hasSubAgentActivity()` is checked before `spinnerActive` in the cascade.
- The `streamingSubAgent` field (used for gutter wrapping sub-agent tool output) is orthogonal to `activeSubAgentEntries` (used for status lines). They serve different purposes and coexist without conflict.
