## Decisions Made

- **Extensionless imports over `.js` ESM convention**: Both tsconfigs already use `"moduleResolution": "bundler"`, making extensionless imports the correct choice for bundler-targeted packages. Direct Node.js ESM consumption is not a target.
- **Fix at codegen level, not output level**: The `.js` extensions were emitted by the Go codegen template. Fixing the template ensures future `make codegen` runs produce clean output automatically.

## Learnings

- Turbopack does not follow the Node.js ESM convention of resolving `.js` → `.ts` for source-only workspace packages consumed via `transpilePackages`
- `"moduleResolution": "bundler"` in tsconfig makes extensionless imports work identically to `.js` imports in `tsc`
- The compiled output (`.js` files in `dist/`) will have extensionless specifiers, which is correct for bundler consumers
