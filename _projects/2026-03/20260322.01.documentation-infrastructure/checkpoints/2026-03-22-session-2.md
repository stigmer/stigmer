## Decisions Made

- **Disabled additional Vale rules** due to conflicts with Stigmer domain terms:
  `Google.Headings` (domain terms must be capitalized in headings),
  `Microsoft.Terms` ("Agent" is not "personal digital assistant"),
  `Microsoft.Avoid` (blocks "backend"), `Microsoft.HeadingPunctuation`
  (question-mark headings like "What is Stigmer?" are standard in docs)
- **Vocabulary additions**: Ollama, ADRs, hardcoding, versionable, runbooks,
  auditable, namespaces, backend, backends, autogenerated
- **Makefile DOCS_SOURCES variable**: Uses `find` with `-path docs/_archive -prune`
  to exclude archive. All five docs targets (lint-docs, lint-docs-audit,
  format-docs, format-docs-check, check-links) share this variable.
- **Em dash style**: Google/Microsoft style requires no spaces around em dashes.
  Adopted throughout new content.
- **Content voice**: Direct, technical, platform-builder audience. Domain terms
  always capitalized. No fluff.

## Learnings

- Vale's `[docs/_archive/**.{md,mdx}]` section with empty `BasedOnStyles` works
  but the `find`-based exclusion in the Makefile is cleaner and more robust
- Microsoft.Terms rule conflicts badly with domain terminology ("Agent" →
  "personal digital assistant") — must be disabled for any project with custom
  domain vocabulary
- Prettier reformats `.mdx` files, which can introduce changes that need a
  re-check against Vale after formatting
