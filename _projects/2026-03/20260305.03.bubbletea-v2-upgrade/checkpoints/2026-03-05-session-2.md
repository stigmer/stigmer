## Decisions Made

- **glamour v2 pseudoversion**: No stable release exists; used `v2.0.0-20260302162937-86f90cfe96d1` per prior research
- **color.Color replacement**: `lipgloss.TerminalColor` was removed in v2; standard `image/color.Color` is the replacement
- **ansi.Strip() for tests**: Lipgloss v2 always emits ANSI codes to any writer (including `bytes.Buffer`); tests that assert on rendered output need ANSI stripping first

## Learnings

- Lipgloss v2 always applies ANSI styling even to non-TTY `io.Writer` destinations (v1 auto-stripped)
- `tea.KeyMsg` in v2 is an interface, not a struct -- always type-assert to `tea.KeyPressMsg`
- `msg.String()` is the idiomatic way to identify keys in v2 (replaces `msg.Type` switch)
- `tea.NewView(content)` is the one-stop constructor for declarative views
