## Decisions Made

- **Rich banned phrases over flat list**: Each entry in `copy-guidelines.json` includes `phrase`, `reason`, and `replacement` (not just a string array). This gives lint tooling and Cursor rules enough context to suggest fixes, not just flag violations.
- **16 banned phrases, not 12**: Extended the original 12 from `website-standards.md` with 4 additional developer-alienating phrases (`leverage`, `best-of-breed`, `cutting-edge`, `AI-powered`) sourced from reminder 006's banned patterns section.
- **Performance budget includes accessibility and SEO thresholds**: Grouped all numerically-enforceable limits (contrast ratios, touch targets, title char limits, OG image dimensions) into a single `performance-budget.json` rather than splitting across multiple files. Rationale: lint tooling needs one file for all measurable gates.
- **Persona definitions in content-requirements.json**: Included the three persona definitions (solo-developer, platform-builder, engineering-leader) with motivations and primary objections, so downstream templates and Cursor rules can reference them without re-reading the IA document.
- **Enforced-by annotations in copy-guidelines.json**: Each voice rule and required pattern includes an `enforced_by` field (`"lint"` or `"review"`) indicating whether it can be automated or requires human/AI review. This guides Phase 8 lint tooling scope.
