## Decisions Made

- **Single queue (no suffix)**: The `:cursor` suffix was a polyglot workaround. Unified runner eliminates it. Temporal routes by activity name within a queue.
- **Fresh start, selective moves**: Did NOT copy cursor-runner wholesale. Started fresh package, moved modules deliberately.
- **Shared vs activity-specific boundary**: mcp-resolver produces `ResolvedMcpServer` (harness-agnostic). Each activity maps it to its SDK format.
- **Go/Java change included in Phase 1**: Trivial diff, reduces risk of later version conflicts.
