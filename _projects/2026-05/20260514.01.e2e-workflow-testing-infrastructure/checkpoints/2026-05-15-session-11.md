## Decisions Made

- **Converter mode mapping**: Proto's flat `{mode, signals[]}` maps to zigflow's discriminated union: `mode:"one"+1→one`, `mode:"one"+N→any`, `mode:"all"→all`. This correctly matches the CNCF SDK's `EventConsumptionStrategy` union type.
