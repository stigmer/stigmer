## Decisions Made

- **Lightweight filter over ResourceWorkbench**: Runners are a small monitoring dataset (~5-20 items), not a searchable catalog. Phase chips + search + sort is proportional. Full workbench deferred.
- **Search threshold at 5 runners**: Search input hidden for small fleets to reduce visual clutter for solo developers.
- **Section heading always visible**: "Organization Runners" heading persists even when empty, with an `EmptyState` inside.
- **Shared utilities in SDK**: `formatRelativeTime`, `PhaseBadge`, `RunnerIcon` live in `@stigmer/react` — both Desktop and web console import from there.
