## Decisions Made

- **Flat 20% native, 10% cursor** chosen over tiered per-model-tier markups. Rationale: simpler to explain, no penalty on economy users, competitive with Cursor's $0.25/M surcharge, sustainable margin.
- **Fallback mechanism** over policy replacement: existing `costTier` values in model registry are preserved for analytics; `BillingPolicyService` falls back to `default` tier when no specific policy is active. This is backward-compatible.
- **costTier retained in registry** — useful for dashboards/reporting even though it no longer drives markup differentiation.
- **New doc is "Explanation" type** per Diataxis, not "Reference" or "How-to" — it explains WHY certain choices save money, not just WHAT the prices are.
