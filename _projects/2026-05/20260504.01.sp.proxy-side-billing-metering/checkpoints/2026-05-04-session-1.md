## Decisions Made

- **Package placement**: `ai.stigmer.proxy.usage` (shared, not in `proxy.llm`) — T03 reuses for Cursor proxy
- **SSE protocol layer separated**: `sse/` sub-package handles wire protocol independently of provider-specific extraction
- **Public visibility on SseFrameDecoder/SseEvent**: Needed because extractors are in parent package; sub-package requires public access
- **Push-based API**: `onBytes(byte[], offset, length)` + `finish()` — designed for tee topology
- **No-op factory pattern**: Unknown providers get a no-op extractor (never null, never throws)
- **JUnit 5 Assertions (not AssertJ)**: Matched existing codebase convention
